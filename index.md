# Privacy Policy for Tracklist Exporter

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

Tracklist Exporter ("the app") is an Android app that lists the audio files on your phone and
exports that list as a text file. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

**In short: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and keeps everything on your phone.**

## What the app accesses

When you tap **Scan**, the app reads information about the audio files stored on your device:
title, artist, album, album artist, genre, year, track number, duration, and the file's location
on your phone. It gets this from Android's media library and, for MP3 files, from the tags inside
the files themselves. To do this it asks for permission to read audio files on your device
(`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 12 and earlier).

## What the app does with it

The information is used only to show you the list inside the app and to create the text file you
ask for with **Export TXT**. The app processes it on your device and does not keep it after you
close the app.

The exported file is saved only in the location you choose in Android's file picker. It contains
the track details listed above. What happens to that file afterwards, for example if you upload or
share it, is up to you.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.
- It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request internet permission.
- It does not use accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- It does not contain third-party tracking code.
- It does not share any information with anyone, including the developer.

## Your control

You can revoke the audio permission at any time in your phone's Settings under Apps, Tracklist
Exporter, Permissions. Uninstalling the app removes it completely. Exported files are ordinary
files that you can delete like any other.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the
date above will change.
